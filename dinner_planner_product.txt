# DinnerPlan: an easier way to plan your meals

Status: planned private beta.

Problem:
Deciding what to cook every evening can feel repetitive. People want a simple way to plan dinners and remember which ingredients to buy.

Planned features:
- A weekly calendar for planning dinners.
- A collection of recipes with ingredients and cooking instructions.
- A shopping list built from the meals selected.
- The ability to change the number of servings.
- An option to save favorite meals.

Audience:
Busy adults and families who cook at home.

Product limitations:
DinnerPlan is a fictional product being prepared for an invite-only beta.
Pricing and a public launch date have not been announced.
Grocery delivery and calorie tracking are not included.
There are no measured savings or customer testimonials.
